Five ways to make a rug.

Hand-Knotted Rugs

Hand-knotted wool rugs, tied row by row for dense detail and lasting character in high-traffic rooms.

Hand-Tufted Rugs

Hand-tufted wool rugs with a softer pile underfoot, made for bedrooms, lounges and everyday comfort.

Hand-Woven Rugs

Flatweave wool and jute rugs, loomed without pile for dining rooms, kitchens and hallway spaces.

Flatweave Rugs

Flatweave rugs are woven without pile for a low-profile finish that works neatly in busy, high-traffic spaces.

Handloom Rugs

Handloom rugs are woven on traditional looms in natural fibres, creating tactile texture with durable everyday structure.
