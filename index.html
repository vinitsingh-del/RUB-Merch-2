Before You Buy

Get the size right the first time.

Living Room

Big enough for the front legs of your sofa and chairs to rest on it, or large enough to hold the whole seating arrangement. Either reads as intentional.

Bedroom

Comfort over drama. A hand-tufted or hand-woven rug feels softer underfoot, right where your feet land each morning.

Dining Room

Add at least 60cm around the table so chairs can be pulled out without catching the edge.

Hallways & Runners

Let a runner follow the natural width of the hallway, with bare floor showing on each side.

Not sure it'll fit? Mark the dimensions out with masking tape before you order. It takes about three minutes and removes the guesswork.
